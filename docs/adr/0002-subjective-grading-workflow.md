# System-proposed, lecturer-approved subjective grading

Subjective questions are graded by the system proposing a grade against the rubric, and the exam's creating lecturer reviewing and approving it. Objective questions are graded automatically at submission with no review.

We rejected fully-automatic grading (no accountability for a rubric judgment call) and fully-manual grading (no benefit from the rubric machinery). The proposal is generated at submission so a lecturer can approve anytime, and the exam cannot close until every subjective answer is approved — keeping "closed means results final" literally true.
