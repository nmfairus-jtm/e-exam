# Closing an exam is blocked until every subjective grade is approved

An exam cannot transition from live to closed while any submitted subjective answer is unapproved. Closing is what makes results final, so grading must be complete first.

The alternative — allowing close with unapproved grades and finishing approvals afterward — was rejected because it splits "results are final" into two moments and lets a closed exam's outcomes still change. The cost is that closing depends on lecturer workload, which is accepted.
